Outer cut line
Makes a new grey line round all the lines: Outline gap = how far outside the lines (0–1 mm hugs them). Tightness 10 follows into every pocket, 1 gives a smooth outline. Holes you made are kept. Drag it with the Cut out tool to fine-tune.
5 · Laser steps
Tap a step to open it. Power is % of your laser max S, speed in mm/min. Written into each .nc file.
Defaults: Engrave lines Vector 25% at 3000 mm/min. Cut Vector 100% at 1000 mm/min, 3 passes, each cut line gets all its passes, holes first. Your changes are kept on this device.
